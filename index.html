A clear player economy

PLAY. PROGRESS. EARN.

CHOME uses two separate reward balances, each with its own purpose.

GAME POINTS

Game Points are the progression currency. Players can collect points through supported game activities, missions and achievements. They help track activity and milestones and are not a cash balance.

CHOX REWARDS

CHOX is a separate reward currency. Eligible games may award CHOX under their published reward rules. Eligible CHOX can be submitted for review through the secure Payout Centre.

Good to know: Game Points and CHOX are not interchangeable. CHOX rewards are not guaranteed in every game or prototype. Any estimated value, eligibility rules, minimums and payout decisions are shown in the authenticated Player Hub and Payout Centre. No payout is promised by simply playing.

PLAYER FAQ

Quick answers about games, accounts and the CHOME reward system.

Do I have to pay to play?

CHOME game discovery and the games marked as free to play do not require a purchase. Any future paid feature will be clearly identified before use.

Do all games award CHOX?

No. CHOX earning is enabled only for games with active, published reward rules. A prototype or playable game may not yet support CHOX rewards.

What is the difference between Game Points and CHOX?

Game Points track gameplay progression. CHOX is a separate reward currency used only where a game's reward rules enable it.

Where can I see my rewards?

Sign in through the Player Hub to view supported account and wallet information. Payout requests, where available, are handled inside the Payout Centre.

Can guests play?

Guests can browse the studio and play games that support guest access. Signing in enables account features such as supported cloud saves and wallet access.

Are all listed games fully released?

No. Each game card displays its current status. Coming-soon, prototype and concept projects are still in development and may change.
